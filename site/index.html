the part that actually matters

Built so you don't have to trust us

Your reproductive health data is some of the most sensitive data you have. Cykel is built so it never leaves your control — and so anyone can verify that claim.

Encrypted on-device

AES-256 encryption with a key derived from your passphrase. Nothing readable is ever written to disk.

Zero network calls

After install, Cykel makes no requests. No analytics, no telemetry, no “anonymous” data collection.

No account, no servers

There's nothing to sign up for and nowhere your data is stored but your own phone. Nothing to subpoena.

Open source

Every line is public. Don't take our word for it — read the code or have someone you trust check it.

good questions

what if i forget my passphrase?

then your data is gone — and we mean really gone. there's no “reset password,” because there's no server holding a copy and no backdoor. that's the whole point. pick something you'll remember, and you can export an encrypted backup any time.

is it actually free?

yes. free, open source, and funded by people who believe reproductive health data should stay private. no ads, no subscription, no “premium” paywall. if you want to chip in, there's a tip jar.

why isn't it in the app store?

app stores require accounts and hand back usage data — the opposite of what cykel is for. installing straight from the web means no store account, no review gatekeeping, and nobody in the middle. on iphone you add it from safari; on android it's one tap.

can you (or anyone) see my data?

no. it's encrypted on your device and there's no server it syncs to. we can't see it, we can't sell it, and we can't be forced to hand over what we never had.
